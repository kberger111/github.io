Employee SMS Consent

How KEB directly invites eligible employees to enroll in its operational text messaging program.

No public enrollment

This page explains KEB's enrollment process. It is not a public sign-up form.

KEB Texting is available only to current KEB Logistics Inc employees and other individuals whom KEB directly invites to enroll. Enrollment is completed through a written or electronic acknowledgment provided directly by KEB during onboarding or through a separate employee enrollment process.

What Participants May Receive

Participants may receive low-volume, recurring, two-way text messages about:

  • Scheduling and availability
  • Attendance and timekeeping questions
  • Route status, service updates, and requests for operational assistance
  • Vehicle, safety, or documentation follow-up
  • Other related employment and operational matters

Messages are not marketing communications.

Consent Language Used by KEB

By signing this acknowledgment, I voluntarily consent to receive recurring employment-related and operational text messages from KEB Logistics Inc at the mobile number I provide. Messages may concern scheduling and availability, attendance and timekeeping, route or service status, requests for assistance, vehicle or safety matters, required documentation, and related employment operations.

I understand that message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. I may reply STOP at any time to discontinue text messages or HELP for assistance. I may also contact ken@keblogistics.com.

I understand that consent to receive text messages is not a condition of employment. If I do not consent or later opt out, I remain responsible for communicating with KEB through another method designated by the company.

I acknowledge that I have access to KEB's SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

The employee enrollment record should include:

  • Employee name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Employee signature or authenticated electronic acceptance
  • Date of consent
  • Version or effective date of the consent language

Withdrawal of Consent

Participants may reply STOP at any time to opt out. After opting out, they may receive one final confirmation. They may reply HELP for assistance or email ken@keblogistics.com.

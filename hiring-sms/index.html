KEB Hiring Texts

Hiring Text Messaging Terms

The terms that apply to KEB’s opt-in text messaging program for job applicants and hiring communications.

Effective date: September 23, 2026

On this siteProgram overviewPrivacy PolicySMS TermsSMS ConsentHiring SMS Terms

Program Purpose

KEB Hiring Texts provides low-volume, two-way messages related to KEB Logistics job applications and hiring. Messages may concern:

  • Application and interview status
  • Scheduling or rescheduling phone or in-person interviews
  • Background check, drug screen, or onboarding next steps
  • Requests for missing application information
  • Other non-marketing hiring communications

This program is not used for advertising or promotional marketing. It is separate from KEB’s employee operational texting program.

Messages may be sent from KEB’s hiring number: (715) 601-6690.

How You Opt In (Consent)

You consent to receive hiring-related text messages from KEB Logistics by doing one of the following:

  • Providing your mobile number on a KEB job application, Indeed/Apply form, or hiring intake form and agreeing to SMS updates; or
  • Texting a hiring keyword (such as JOBS or APPLY) to (715) 601-6690; or
  • Replying YES (or similarly confirming) after KEB texts you about a specific open role or application you started.

Consent is voluntary and is not required to apply for a job. If you do not opt in, KEB may still contact you by email, phone call, or other non-SMS methods when appropriate.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies with your application status. Typical volume is a few messages per week while an application is active; some applicants receive fewer.

Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan. KEB does not charge a separate fee for this program.

Opting Out

STOPOpt out at any time.
HELPGet program assistance.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Standard opt-out keywords such as CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, OPTOUT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or STOPALL may also be recognized.

After an opt-out, KEB may send one final confirmation that messaging has stopped. KEB will not send further hiring-program texts unless you later provide new express consent.

Opting out of texts does not withdraw your job application by itself. You can still finish the hiring process through email or phone.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance, or email ken@keblogistics.com.

Privacy

Information collected through KEB Hiring Texts is handled under KEB’s Privacy Policy.

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Delivery and Availability

Delivery depends on mobile-network availability and is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely on SMS for emergencies.

Changes

KEB may update these terms as the program or legal requirements change. Updated terms will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If a material change affects the subject of your consent, KEB will obtain any additional consent required before messaging under the changed program.

Privacy Policy

How KEB handles information from website visitors and participants in the employee text messaging program.

Effective date: July 23, 2026

On this siteProgram overviewPrivacy PolicySMS TermsSMS Consent

KEB Logistics Inc (“KEB,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of visitors to this website and participants in the KEB employee text messaging program. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with KEB, we may collect:

  • Your name, mobile phone number, email address, and employee-identification information
  • Records of your consent to receive text messages, including the date, method, and version of the consent form
  • Text messages and responses exchanged with KEB
  • Delivery status, opt-out status, timestamps, and related messaging records
  • Operational information relevant to a message, such as scheduling, attendance, timekeeping, route status, requests for assistance, vehicle matters, or required documentation
  • Information you voluntarily provide by email or through another direct communication with KEB

This website is informational and does not provide a public enrollment form for the employee text messaging program.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Communicate with employees about scheduling, attendance, timekeeping, route operations, requests for assistance, service status, vehicles, safety, documentation, and related employment operations
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Maintain records of consent, messages, responses, delivery status, and opt-outs
  • Review and resolve operational or timekeeping questions
  • Protect the security and integrity of KEB systems and records
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and recordkeeping obligations

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Privacy commitment

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent records will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

KEB does not sell or rent personal information.

KEB may disclose mobile information to service providers that help us operate the messaging program—such as telecommunications, messaging, hosting, security, and recordkeeping providers—but only as necessary for them to provide services to KEB. Those providers are not authorized to use the information for their own marketing.

Message frequency varies based on operational needs. Message and data rates may apply. Participants may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Other Disclosures

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers working on KEB's behalf
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, property, or security of KEB, its employees, or others
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of the business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements

Data Retention

KEB retains information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operational, employment, payroll, audit, legal, and compliance needs. Consent and opt-out records may be retained after messaging ends so KEB can document and honor the individual's communication preferences.

Security

KEB uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may opt out of KEB text messages at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, you may receive one final confirmation message. KEB will use another designated communication method when appropriate.

For help with the text messaging program, reply HELP or email ken@keblogistics.com.

Children's Privacy

This website and the employee text messaging program are not directed to children under 13, and KEB does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through them.

Changes to This Policy

KEB may update this policy from time to time. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to ken@keblogistics.com.

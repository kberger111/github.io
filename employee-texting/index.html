Employee Texting Program

A low-volume, two-way communication channel for routine work coordination with current KEB employees.

On this siteProgram overviewPrivacy PolicySMS TermsSMS Consent
Program scope

KEB Texting is an opt-in operational program. Messages are not marketing communications or public solicitations.

What the program supports

KEB Logistics Inc uses text messaging to coordinate routine employment-related and operational matters with current employees who have expressly consented to participate.

  • Scheduling and availability
  • Attendance and timekeeping questions
  • Route status, service updates, and requests for assistance
  • Vehicle, safety, or documentation follow-up
  • Other related employment and operational matters

Enrollment is voluntary

Employees enroll by providing their mobile number and signing a written or electronic SMS consent acknowledgment supplied directly by KEB. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of employment. This website does not provide public enrollment.

Employees who decline SMS consent or later opt out remain responsible for communicating through another method designated by KEB.

Frequency, rates, and choices

Message frequency varies based on operational needs. Message and data rates may apply. KEB does not charge a separate program fee.
